# SCOUT Report: Teacher Store Niche

## What's selling
Shirts (esp. Comfort Colors-brand) dominate, then mugs, totes, personalized items. **Subject-specific humor** wins (grammar/English puns, math jokes, history puns). **"Teacher life" identity items** and **end-of-year appreciation gifts** ("Thank You For Helping Me Grow," "It's In The Syllabus") both sell. **Personalized "Mrs./Mr. [Name]" retro shirts** and **grade/department matching-shirt orders** are recurring bestsellers.

## Saturated vs. open
**Avoid:** generic "Teacher" / "Teacher Life" text-only designs — too many weak competitors.
**Room:** hyper-specific sub-niches — subject + grade band ("5th grade math teacher," "SPED teacher," "kindergarten teacher"), life-stage crossovers ("first-year teacher," "retiring teacher"), personalized name variants.

## Visual style
Retro/bootleg-band-tee layout but sharpened — cleaner type hierarchy, controlled/muted vintage colors, not loud clipart. Comfort Colors' soft washed aesthetic is a strong buyer signal. Secondary: cottagecore/soft-illustration (apples, chalkboards) for a gentler look.

## Price points (directional — not independently confirmed with hard sales data)
Shirts ~$25-30. Mugs ~$15-22. Personalization commands a premium.

## Top 6 concepts
1. Subject-specific pun tee series (grammar/math/history), sharpened retro badge
2. Grade-band identity tee ("Kindergarten Teacher," "5th Grade Teacher")
3. "It's In The Syllabus" mug
4. "Thank You For Helping Me/Us Grow" mug/tote (personalizable, end-of-year gift)
5. Personalized "Mrs./Mr. [Name], Est. [Year]" retro tee
6. "Teacher, Retiring [Year]" tee/mug

**SCOUT's recommendation:** skip generic "Teacher Life" entirely, launch with subject/grade-specific retro-badge shirts paired with 1-2 funny/sentimental mugs.
