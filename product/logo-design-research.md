# Research: What Top Fiverr Logo Sellers Actually Deliver

## What top sellers deliver
Flat vector marks — clean lines, solid colors (1-3 max). 3D/rendered logos are a separate niche, used as a bonus mockup on top of a flat master, never as the only file. High ratings correlate with portfolio consistency and clear concept storytelling, not visual flashiness.

## Core principles: pro vs. amateur/AI-looking
Simple, original, versatile, scalable, balanced, timeless.
- **Scalability:** must read correctly from favicon (16px) to billboard size
- **Versatility:** must work as solid black silhouette, in reverse/white, in single ink color (embroidery/stamps/screen printing), and as a small flat app icon
- **Simplicity:** recognizable by shape alone, no reliance on effects

**Glow/neon/3D/particle effects are considered amateur or AI tells** because: they fail the black-silhouette test, print as fuzzy halos in screen printing, look blocky/undefined in embroidery, can't cleanly vectorize (gradients/lighting produce messy paths), and disappear on non-dark backgrounds. Industry commentary explicitly flags "raster effects on what should be a vector deliverable" as the clearest AI/unfinished-logo signal.

## Package contents at top tier
Multiple initial concepts, revision rounds, final files in PNG/JPG/AI/EPS/PDF **and SVG (vector)**. Full deliverable set: primary lockup, icon-only mark, full-color, black version, white/reverse version, single-color version. Premium tiers add brand guidelines and social/stationery mockups.

## Pricing
Basic ($10-30): 1 concept, limited revisions, PNG/JPG only, no vector file.
Premium ($75-150+): multiple concepts, unlimited revisions, full vector source files, all lockup variations.
**The differentiator is file completeness and variation count, not visual complexity of the mark.**

## Applied to our compass+arrow concept
The concept (architect's compass merged with an ascending arrow/chart line) is sound and ownable. The execution (heavy glow/neon/particle effects) was the problem. Rebuilt as: flat, closed-path, 1-2 solid colors, no gradients/glow/bevel/shadow. Tested against the black-silhouette version — see `org/brand-standards.md`'s Logos & Marks section for the standing standard this created.
