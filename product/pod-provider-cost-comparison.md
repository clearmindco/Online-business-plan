# THE MECHANIC: POD Provider Cost Comparison — Mugs & Shirts

**Caveat:** aggregated from 2026 comparison sources, not live provider dashboards — directional, not exact. All four providers are free to browse real pricing by creating an account.

| Provider | Mug base cost | Shirt base cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Printify** | ~$3.50-4.69 | ~$9.04-10.36 | Cheapest on both, open marketplace creates price competition |
| **Printful** | ~$8-13 | ~$11.69 | Most consistent shipping (owns facilities) |
| **Gelato** | ~$8.25 | ~$9.80-11.50 | Best for international (32 countries local production) |
| **SPOD** | mid-range | competitive | Shopify-native, 1-day production, no subscription |

**Subscriptions:** none of the premium tiers (Printful Growth $24.99/mo, Printify Premium $24.99-39/mo, Gelato+ $19.99/mo) pay for themselves until real volume (~$1,500-2,000+/mo in COGS). Start free-tier everywhere.

## THE MECHANIC's recommendation
**Start with Printify's free plan** as the single Shopify-integrated provider for both mugs and shirts across all niches — cheapest on both anchor products, one integration instead of several, zero cost until sales justify upgrading. Add Gelato later only if international shipping speed becomes a real bottleneck for a specific niche.
