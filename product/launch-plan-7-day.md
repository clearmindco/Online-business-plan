# 7-Day Launch Plan — Wealth Architect System

Goal: first dollar within 48 hours. This is a content/creator + digital product play — audience-first is a myth here, you don't need an audience to sell a $19 impulse product into an existing niche.

## Day 0 (today, ~90 minutes)

1. **Host the product** — Gumroad or Stan Store. Both approve instantly, no wait. Upload `wealth-architect-system.md` as a PDF (convert with any free markdown-to-PDF tool) or a Notion page link.
2. **Set price:** $19 core offer, per the offer doc.
3. **Paste sales copy** from `offer-and-sales-page.md` directly into the product description.
4. **Turn on the 7-day guarantee** in the platform's refund settings.

## Day 1 — Distribution, organic (zero ad spend)

- Post 1 short-form video (Reel/TikTok/Short) using the same hook style as the post that inspired this: "This AI prompt turns ChatGPT into your personal wealth advisor" — record yourself running Phase 1 live on screen with a blurred/example profile, show the output, CTA to the product link in bio.
- Post the same angle as a Twitter/X thread: hook tweet + screenshots of one phase's output + link.
- Drop it in 2–3 relevant subreddits or FB groups where self-promotion is allowed (check rules first — don't get banned, that kills the channel for future launches).

## Day 2 — Direct outreach

- DM 10 people in your existing network who've talked about side income, budgeting, or "wanting to get better with money." Not a pitch — "I built this, thought of you, here's the link" is enough.

## Day 3–4 — Iterate on what worked

- Whichever channel got the first click/sale, double down: post again, same format, new hook angle.
- If zero traction: the hook is the problem, not the product. Rewrite the opening line and re-post — don't touch the product yet.

## Day 5–7 — Systematize

- Once you have 3+ sales, write down exactly what worked (which platform, which hook, which CTA). That becomes your SOP for the next digital product launch — this is the `sop-builder` moment: capture it once, reuse it every time you ship the next offer.
- Start soft-pitching the $197 "Done-For-You Run" upsell in DMs to anyone who bought the $19 version and engaged with it.

## What NOT to do

- Don't wait for a logo, a "real" website, or a bigger audience before launching. The offer is the product — ship it ugly, fix it after first sale.
- Don't spend on ads before you have at least 3 organic sales proving the offer converts.

## Next link in the chain

Offer → **Traffic** → Convert → Retain → Automate → Scale.
You're standing at Traffic right now. Once you have repeatable sales, the next skill to pull in is `conversion-rate-optimization` on the sales page itself, and `email-sequence` to capture buyers into a list for the $197 upsell and future products.
