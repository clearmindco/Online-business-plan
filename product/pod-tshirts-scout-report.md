# SCOUT Report: Print-on-Demand T-Shirt Landscape (Sept 2026)

**Caveat up front:** Etsy/POD platforms don't publish real sales-volume data. This is directional (listing density, seller trend reports, search-intent patterns from Printify/Printful/Etsy/Kittl/Everbee), not confirmed revenue numbers. Treat it as a strong starting signal, not proof.

## Niches: pursue vs. avoid

| Niche | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Money-mindset / financial-literacy apparel | **Pursue** | Real demand exists, but current sellers are small/unbranded — under-served by anyone with actual authority, not crowded |
| Digital-nomad / financial-independence lifestyle | **Pursue (secondary)** | Named 2026 emerging niche, shares our existing audience, zero new content needed |
| Personalized name/date life-moment shirts | Pursue, but only with genuine personalization | Generic versions are oversaturated; personalized ones aren't |
| Pet memorial / pet-parent identity | Pursue (not relevant to us) | High emotional specificity, fragmented competition |
| Retro/hustle-culture "entrepreneur" tees | **Avoid** | Explicitly flagged as one of the oversaturated categories; sample listings had minimal traction |
| Celestial/astrology, AI-worker humor | Pursue cautiously (not relevant to us) | Real trends, outside our authority |

## Winning visual style right now
Minimalist/clean line art, bold simple typography (short slogans, not paragraphs), restrained 2-3 color palettes, premium mockup styling — not flat clipart. Illustrated/cartoon styles are fading relative to typography-led minimalism.

## Pricing
$19.99-$24.99 standard tee on Etsy (15-25% premium over Amazon Merch). Charm pricing (.99) still wins. Bundles ("2 for $39.99") lift order value. Target 30-50% net margin. Q4/seasonal capsule drops are a real growth lever — fits our existing "drop" structure.

## SCOUT's recommendation
Launch a small capsule of minimalist, bold-typography "money mindset" tees using **actual Wealth Architect brand lines**, not generic hustle slogans — leverages brand authority a from-scratch competitor doesn't have.
