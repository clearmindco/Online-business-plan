# The Master Context Prompt — for ChatGPT (or any outside AI)

Reusable, not a one-off. Whenever PROTON needs to bring an outside tool up to speed on the whole business before showing it something (a video, an idea, a new concept), paste the block below first. Update this file when the business materially changes (new BU, new live product, new phase) so it never goes stale — same standing-rule discipline as everything else in this repo.

---

## PASTE THIS INTO CHATGPT

```
I'm going to give you full context on my business before I show you anything else, so you actually understand what you're looking at instead of guessing. Read this whole thing first. After this, I'm going to share a video — use everything below to understand exactly what I'm trying to do and respond to it with that context, not generically.

WHO I AM AND WHY THIS EXISTS

My name is Javier. I have an LLC and EIN already formed. My goal is financial freedom — not "comfortable," actually free: able to fully support myself, my family, and eventually my parents, without trading time for money to survive. After that, the goal is to help close friends get there too. After that, use what's been built to give back at the largest scale possible — the stated ambition is to become one of the biggest providers of food and water in the world, if that's achievable.

This isn't a vague mission statement. Every decision in this business gets measured against it. The first concrete milestone is the first real sale — not a pretty pipeline, not a complete org chart, an actual dollar from an actual stranger. After that, the whole operation stops and reviews what worked before scaling further.

HOW THIS IS STRUCTURED

I run this like a company with a virtual leadership team instead of doing every layer myself:

- CEO — capital allocation across all business lines and trading, go/no-go calls, final say when teams disagree. Recommendation-first, not a menu of options.
- Chief of Staff — turns decisions into execution, tracks the master task list, unblocks bottlenecks, documents SOPs as they're built.
- CMO — growth across everything: brand, content, paid, SEO, conversion, positioning. One growth engine, not separate marketing efforts per line.

Under that, FOUR business lines run in parallel, plus a fifth track that's kept deliberately separate:

1. SaaS & AI Tools — recurring revenue, churn is the enemy.
2. Ecommerce / Physical Products — currently building a multi-niche print-on-demand operation (money-mindset apparel, mom coffee mugs, a teacher-specific store, dog-breed-specific niches, a premium illustrated-graphic tier). Research done on all niches; real spend (samples, ads) requires my explicit go-ahead before it happens, always.
3. Freelance / Agency — speed to revenue, productize fast, remove myself from delivery.
4. Content / Creator — audience first, monetize second, own the email list. This is the most active line right now.
5. Trading Desk (separate, not folded into the 4 lines — different proof standard, different timeline) — see below.

WHAT'S ACTUALLY LIVE RIGHT NOW (real, not projected)

Content/Creator line, brand "The Wealth Architect" (Instagram: @thewealtharchitecthq_):
- The Wealth Architect System — live on Gumroad, $5.99 entry guide.
- The Generational Wealth Prompt Vault — live on Gumroad, $27, a curated 70-prompt AI library across 7 wealth-building categories, positioned as the upgrade from the entry guide.
- The Savings Architect — live on Gumroad, $6.99, a 6-chapter savings-system guide.
- Instagram account is active with a real posting cadence (5 posts/week), scheduled through Metricool with auto-publish, using a comment-to-DM mechanic for the paid upgrade products.
- Real, honest number: total revenue across every product right now is $0. Nothing has sold yet. This business never states a projection as if it were a result — only confirmed numbers get logged anywhere.

Everything else (POD apparel/mugs/teacher/dog-breed niches, SaaS, freelance/agency) is in research or design phase, not yet live, no real spend committed.

BRAND STANDARD EVERY PRODUCT IS HELD TO

The bar is "100x the top-selling comparable product in that category," not "good enough." Tone is high-energy, direct-address, momentum-driven — the reader should feel something is happening to them, never a dry manual. Every product name reads like an insider system ("The [X] Architect System"), not a generic guide. Covers have to trigger "I need this, I want to be that person" — aspirational and cinematic, never flat corporate, never real people's likenesses. No invented statistics or unsourced claims ever — every factual claim is either sourced or clearly hedged as general guidance, with a standard disclaimer on finance content ("not financial, tax, or legal advice"). Writing avoids unnecessary em dashes and anything that reads like an AI pattern-generator instead of a person talking. Products and posts never confess that AI is running them — the AI is a tool that teaches value to the audience, not a fact about the business that gets revealed to customers.

THE TRADING SYSTEM (separate track, its own rules)

I'm also building a systematic stock trading approach with the explicit goal of generating real income first, then selling the documented, proven system as a product later — never the other way around.

Three phases, never skipped:
- Phase 1 (now): build and backtest only. No real money until a strategy has a documented edge across a minimum of 100 trades or 6 months of data, with exact entry/exit criteria and position sizing written down clearly enough for a stranger to reproduce.
- Phase 2 (months 3-6): live trading at small, defined position sizes. Track win rate, average R, max drawdown, Sharpe ratio, monthly return.
- Phase 3 (months 6-12): productize only after real income is confirmed across at least two different market conditions, with real brokerage statements as proof.

The system never gets marketed, pre-sold, or launched before there's a minimum of 6 months of live trading results with real money, positive expectancy confirmed, documented drawdown, and rules a stranger could follow without help.

Macro awareness runs continuously underneath every trading decision: TLT (bond flows / risk-on-off), DXY (dollar strength), VIX (fear/volatility), oil/energy, the 2yr/10yr yield curve, and credit spreads (HYG vs LQD) all get watched before any position, not just when asked.

WHAT I NEED FROM YOU

Hold all of this. When I share the video next, respond to it with this full picture in mind — what business line it touches, whether it fits the brand standard above, whether it moves the actual mission forward, and whether it's a real opportunity or a distraction from what's already in motion. Don't restate this back to me — just use it.
```
