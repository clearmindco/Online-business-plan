# Founder Context

This file provides context about the business that the `founder-skills` pack (`.claude/skills/`) uses to personalize its output. Filled in from the real repo state in `org/mission.md`, `org/brand-standards.md`, and `org/operating-model.md` — not placeholder text.

## About Your Business

- **Company name**: Wealth Architect HQ (also operating the "[X] Architect" guide line on Gumroad — Wealth Architect System, Savings Architect, Dating Architect, etc.)
- **Industry**: Faceless digital content and products — personal finance/self-improvement guides on Gumroad, Instagram growth (@thewealtharchitect), with print-on-demand (apparel/mugs) and a stock-trading product line in earlier stages.
- **Target audience**: People who feel behind financially and want a real, structured system instead of generic advice — direct-address, no-BS tone, identity-driven ("the version of you that has it together").
- **Value proposition**: Real, narrow, actionable frameworks (named, memorable systems like "The Wealth Split," "The Debt Freedom Ladder") instead of broad generic finance content — held to a "100x the top Gumroad seller" quality bar, not "good enough."

## Brand Voice

- **Tone**: High-energy, direct-address ("you"), momentum-driven. Never dry/textbook, never academic hedging.
- **Personality traits**: Disciplined, insider, aspirational, no-nonsense.
- **Key messages**: Small consistent choices compound (the Slight Edge philosophy). Systems over willpower. You don't need a better plan, you need to keep going.
- **Words to use**: "system," "framework," "architect," direct second-person.
- **Words to avoid**: Unnecessary em dashes (write like a person talking, not a pattern-generator). Never confess that content/pages are AI-run — teach the tool, never reveal the operation. No invented statistics or unsourced claims — hedge or cite real sources.

## Business Goals

- **Short-term (3-6 months)**: Land the first real sale across any product; prove the content/Gumroad pipeline; grow @thewealtharchitect from near-zero followers with daily posting.
- **Long-term (1-3 years)**: Multiple real income streams (content/creator, ecommerce/POD, AI tools/SaaS, freelance/agency, and a documented stock-trading system) adding up to real financial freedom for the founder and family.
- **Key metrics**: Real confirmed revenue per product (tracked in `org/revenue-ledger.md`, never a projection), Instagram follower/engagement growth, Gumroad units sold.

## Products/Services

- **Main offerings**: The Wealth Architect System ($5.99), The Savings Architect ($6.99), The Dating Architect ($6.99), The Generational Wealth Prompt Vault ($27) — all on Gumroad. Print-on-demand apparel line in design phase. AI Tools/SaaS and Affiliate fronts newly activated (research phase).
- **Pricing model**: One-time digital download purchases (Gumroad), no subscriptions yet.
- **Key features/benefits**: Narrow, single-problem guides that outsell broad ones; real frameworks, not generic advice; built and QA'd through an explicit research → write → design → QA pipeline before anything ships.

## Competitors

- **Main competitors**: Generic personal-finance Gumroad guides, pickup-artist/seduction-style dating guides (explicitly avoided — see `product/dating-architect.md`'s angle note), broad "finance 101" content.
- **Your advantages**: Narrow, named frameworks with real research behind every topic choice (see `org/gumroad-content-engine.md`'s Topic Queue, every entry backed by a cited demand signal); a documented quality bar (`org/brand-standards.md`) applied to every drop by default.
